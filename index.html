Who we are

Turning complex challenges into practical solutions.

KINGS ENVIRONMENTAL CONSULTING LIMITED is a Nigerian environmental management, monitoring and evaluation, research and consultancy services firm. We support public institutions, development programmes, project implementation units, private organisations and communities in addressing environmental, social and development challenges.

Our work brings together environmental investigation, applied research, socioeconomic assessment, spatial information and evidence-based reporting to strengthen decision-making and promote sustainable development.

What we do

Integrated services for environmental and development priorities.

We provide technical and professional support across the project cycle—from initial assessment and data collection to monitoring, evaluation and actionable recommendations.

◉

Monitoring & Evaluation

Baseline, routine monitoring, process reviews, mid-term and end-line evaluations with measurable indicators and verifiable findings.

⌕

Environmental Assessment

Environmental baseline studies, sampling where required, compliance monitoring, erosion and drainage assessments, and impact management recommendations.

▤

Baseline & Statistical Surveys

Household, community, institutional and environmental surveys, from study design and sampling to field supervision and reporting.

⌖

GIS & Spatial Analysis

GPS field data collection, spatial databases, project mapping, land-use interpretation and geospatial analysis for technical reports.

♧

Social & Socioeconomic Studies

Livelihoods, vulnerability, stakeholder engagement, gender and social inclusion, community assets, health and safety considerations.

⌁

Ecology & Vegetation Studies

Vegetation inventory, habitat observations, land-cover interpretation and ecological baseline documentation.

◫

Data Management & Reporting

Data validation, quantitative and qualitative analysis, indicator matrices and clear, traceable technical reports.

◎

Stakeholder Engagement

Structured consultations with institutions, community leadership, women and youth groups and other relevant stakeholders.

⌂

Watershed & Restoration

Watershed management, soil erosion assessment and environmental restoration monitoring support.
